Frequently Asked Questions

What does a dental implants periodontist do?

A periodontist is trained in the gums and supporting bone, so a dental implants periodontist can place implants, treat gum disease and rebuild bone for a stable, long lasting result.

Are same day dental implants right for everyone?

Not always. Bone quality, gum health and overall health decide whether one day smile restoration is safe. Your implant consultation and 3D scan will confirm this.

Can you fix a failed dental implant?

In many cases yes. Dental implant rescue and dental implant revision can correct infection, poor placement or bone loss, depending on your imaging.

What is the difference between All on 4 and implant supported dentures?

All on 4 dental implants are fixed in place as permanent teeth. Implant supported dentures can be removable or fixed, and your plan depends on bone, budget and goals.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, titanium implants can last for decades, which is why they are considered a long lasting tooth replacement option.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and credit cards. Ask our team about financing and treatment plans during your visit.

Full Arch and All on 4 Dental Implants

Full arch dental implants replace an entire row of missing teeth using as few as four strategically placed implants. All on 4 dental implants give you a fixed, permanent teeth solution that does not slip, click or need adhesive. This implant dentistry approach often avoids extensive bone grafting by angling posterior implants into denser bone. Your new smile is custom designed for appearance and function, so eating, speaking and laughing feel natural again. It is one of the most popular missing teeth solutions for patients tired of failing teeth or loose dentures, and a long lasting dental implants option with a clear, predictable treatment path.

Why Choose a Periodontist for Implants?

Periodontists complete additional training in gum disease, bone regeneration and surgical placement. That matters because gum disease is a leading cause of tooth loss and also a leading cause of implant complications. By treating infection, reshaping tissue and rebuilding bone when needed, we create a stable base for implant surgery. The result is a restoration that looks natural at the gum line and stays healthy for years.

Every treatment begins with an implant consultation. We review your history, take digital scans and discuss tooth replacement options in plain language. You will understand the implant procedure, expected healing, maintenance and cost before you decide. If you need dental restoration for several teeth, we coordinate each stage so you are never left guessing what comes next.

Your Treatment Journey

First, we meet for a consultation and imaging. Second, we create a personalized plan covering implant surgery, healing and your final teeth.
